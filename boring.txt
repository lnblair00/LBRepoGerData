How to Run the App
Download the app -> 
Once downloaded, simply double click and the app will automatically open in your default browser.
Enter the password to access the map: MRL_GerFacMap_2025!x
When you're done searching, simply close the browser tab. The app will automatically shut down in the background — no processes remain running.

Using the Interactive Map
Facility Groups
Tick one or more groups:
(P) Pharmacies
(H) Hospitals
(R) Rehabs
(D) Physicians
Only selected groups will appear on the map.

Filters
Each facility type has its own expandable section where you can filter by:
VCA status (for pharmacies)
VCA Type (for VCA pharmacies)
Operator type (hospitals & rehabs)
Facility type (hospitals & rehabs)
Specialities (hospitals & physicians)
All filters support:
Multi-select
Search inside dropdowns
Collapsible panels

Proximity Filter
You can show pharmacies within 1-25 km of any visible hospitals, physicians, or rehabs:
Tick any non-pharmacy facility group
Enable: “Show pharmacies within X km of selected facilities”
Adjust the radius slider (1–25 km)
Exporting Data
Click Export to Excel to download the full list of currently visible facilities as a .xlsx file.

Troubleshooting
App does not open automatically: Open your browser and go to: http://127.0.0.1:5000
Antivirus warns about the EXE: This is normal with unsigned internal apps; select “Allow”
Map loads slowly on first run: Large facility datasets are loading; subsequent use is instant
Filters seem unresponsive: Click Reset Filters and re-select your options
